Smart Mind – Support

A note app that reminds you at the right place and time.

What Smart Mind does

Write a note once and Smart Mind brings it back when it matters. It sorts each note into a to-do, errand, idea or event, and picks up the place and time from what you wrote ("buy milk when I get home", "call mom at 6pm"). Save your places (Home, Uni, Work, the gym…) on the map, and the app alerts you when you arrive there. For errands, it can also alert you when you're at a nearby supermarket, pharmacy or post office.

Common questions

  • I don't get arrival alerts. In Settings, turn on automatic location and allow location Always and notifications. Each place needs a location: open the place and use your current location, search an address, pick it on the map or paste a link.
  • How do I delete my account? Settings → Delete account. See how to delete your account for details and how to ask by email.
