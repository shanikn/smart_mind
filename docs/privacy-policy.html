Smart Mind – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Smart Mind is a note app that reminds you of the right notes at the right place and time. It is a personal project. This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

Questions: shanik867@gmail.com

What we collect and why

  • Your account. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. It stores your email address and a user ID. If you sign in with Google, Firebase also receives the basic profile Google shares (name and profile photo). On iPhone you can also sign in with Apple: Apple shares your email, or, if you choose "Hide My Email", a random relay address (…@privaterelay.appleid.com) that forwards to you, and, the first time only, the name you allow. If you sign in with email and password, your password is handled by Firebase; we never see it. Our server receives only your user ID, to keep your data separate from everyone else's.
  • Your notes. The text you write, plus details the app adds or you set: the kind of note (to-do, errand, idea, event), a place, a time or date, a folder, whether alerts are on, and your feedback on reminders. To find the notes that matter right now, our server also computes a numerical representation of each note's text (an "embedding") and stores it with the note.
  • Your saved places. Place names (Home, Uni, Gym…) and keywords are stored on our server so notes can be linked to them. A place's location, address and radius stay on your phone; they are not sent to our server.
  • Your location. If you allow it, Smart Mind collects location data to alert you when you arrive at your saved places or are near stores for your errands, even when the app is closed or not in use. This happens on your phone; your location history is not stored or uploaded, and your places' coordinates stay on the phone. An approximate position leaves the phone only to find nearby stores and to rank address search results (below). It is not saved to disk or to our database; the server keeps the rounded position in memory for up to a few hours, only to avoid repeating the same lookup:
    • To find stores near you for your open errands: your position, rounded on the phone to about 100 m, is sent to our server, which asks OpenStreetMap's Overpass service.
    • When you pick a point on the map or paste coordinates or a Google Maps link: that point (or link) is sent to our server to find its address.
  • Address searches. The words you search for are sent to our server, which looks them up with Google Places (Google Maps Platform) or OpenStreetMap Nominatim. So that nearby results come first, the app also sends your phone's approximate location (rounded to about 1 km), but only if the phone already has one from the last 10 minutes; it never turns on location just for a search. This location is not saved to disk or to our database; the server keeps it in memory for a few minutes, only to avoid repeating the same search. Short Google Maps links you paste are opened by our server only to read the coordinates in them.
  • Maps. The map screen loads map images directly from OpenStreetMap's tile servers and the map library from unpkg.com. Like any website, these services see your IP address and which map area is shown.
  • Settings on your phone. Preferences such as colors, appearance, alert options and your current place are stored only on your phone.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools in the app.
  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Reminders are created on your phone; we don't use a push-notification service.
  • Our server logs record note IDs and technical errors, not the text of your notes.

Where your data is stored

  • Notes and place names: a MongoDB Atlas database.
  • Our server, including the note embeddings: Microsoft Azure.
  • Your sign-in account: Google Firebase.

Data travels between the app and these services over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each user can only reach their own notes and places.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. You can delete single notes and places in the app at any time.

Delete your account: in the app, open Settings → Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. This deletes all your notes, your places, their copies on our server, and your sign-in account, and clears the app's data on your phone. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more, and the app's access to your Apple ID is revoked too. You can also ask us by email; see how to delete your account.

Copies in our hosting providers' backups, if any, are removed when those backups expire.

Children

Smart Mind is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
